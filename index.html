About

Seoul in New York is a non-profit film production company. We develop and produce original short films built for the festival circuit, and we fund them through the generosity of people who believe a small film can go a long way.

Our stories live in the space between two cities: immigrants, strangers, families, and the languages that pass between them. We shoot at the edges of the day, work with small crews, and spend every dollar on what ends up on screen.

Every project is chosen for one reason: it deserves a room full of people watching it in the dark.

A short film · Written & directed by Minae Kim · Co-produced by Minae Kim and Arshin Jain

One evening, two strangers meet on the bridge that joins Queens and Manhattan. A quiet study of distance, memory, and the city between two immigrants, shot at the edges of the day.

Founder

Minae Elena Kim 김민애

Actress · Director · Producer

Trained in psychology, disciplined by judo, sharpened on stand-up stages in New York, Los Angeles, and London. She is drawn to characters who say less than they mean.

Her credits include Netflix's Kinda Pregnant, the Amazon Prime feature Indian Cowboy, and Green Night, which premiered at the Berlinale and screened at the Busan International Film Festival. On the Queensboro Bridge is her first short film as writer and director.
